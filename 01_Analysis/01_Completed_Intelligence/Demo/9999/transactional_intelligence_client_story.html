Client Conversation Guide

1. Start with data confidence and what the file coverage can support.
2. Explain the top relationship behavior, not just the top chart.
3. Recommend one to three action plays with owners, channels, and measurement.
4. Agree on campaign or banker execution and the next monthly outcome readout.

Decision Questions

  1. Which play is most aligned to the client's current growth or retention priority?
  2. Who owns activation: marketing, branch leadership, banker team, or CSM follow-up?
  3. What suppressions, compliance exclusions, or branch assignments are required before launch?
  4. How will outcomes be captured back into next month's run?
