Overall Engine Process

1. Monthly file dropClient sends account, transaction, and support files. Current demo has 6 CSV files.
2. Layout intelligenceProfile source columns, match contracts, expose missing mappings, and create data-team actions.
3. Feature martBuild account-month behavior for balances, credits, spend, payroll, external FI, and activity.
4. Analysis domainsMap 20+ repo sections into 8 business intelligence domains and 30 section reviews.
5. Strategy outputsCreate opportunities, scorecards, roadmap, action plan, campaign targets, and role handoffs.
6. Measurement loopCompare next-period behavior to prior targets and turn results into the consulting story.
