Executive Questions

The monthly decision set

Who are active relationships?Open, eligible, engaged, dormant, personal, business, debit-enabled, payroll-linked.
Where is money moving?Merchants, MCCs, channels, transaction types, competitors, and payment ecosystems.
Who owns the relationship?Primary, split, or secondary based on payroll, balances, spend, deposits, and recurring activity.
Where is value leaking?Revenue leakage, deposit flight, attrition, competitor capture, interchange gaps, Reg E opportunity.
What should we do next?Target population, message, offer, channel, measurement plan.
Can we prove it?Lineage, coverage, quality, confidence, and source section evidence.
