How We Classify — The Detail

Not all "competitor" spend means the same thing.

This is the distinction that keeps the numbers honest. Every counterparty gets a role, and each role drives a different play. Mixing them is what inflates the story.

Competitor FI

Chase · BofA · local · CU

A real banking relationship. Drives PFI displacement, wallet share, win-back. Strongest on ACH / check.

FS Product

Auto · mortgage · invest

A product held elsewhere = cross-sell / leakage. Reported in aggregate; credit stays report-only.

Ecosystem

Venmo · Cash App · Zelle

A rail, not a destination. Its own disintermediation signal — never counted as a competitor.

Merchant

Everyday spend

Context & life-stage color (pet, moving, travel) — supporting signal, never a target alone.

What We Can & Can't See — Be Honest Up Front

The scope the team needs to know.

These are the guardrails that keep us credible with a bank's compliance team — and stop us overselling internally.

We can see

  • Outbound spend & transfers — card, ACH, check
  • The counterparty on each (merchant / institution)
  • Balance held with us (Avg / Curr Bal)
  • Tenure, age, product, personal vs. business
  • Card engagement, trends, and campaign response history

We can't see

  • Inbound deposits / paychecks — primacy is inferred, not observed
  • A member ID linking one person's accounts — grain is the account
  • Where cash withdrawals ultimately go
  • The true destination behind an ecosystem transfer
  • Fields vary by client — a signal fires only when its inputs are present

Two deliberate choices: wallet share is coverage-corrected (we abstain when coverage is thin), and lending is report-only (no individual credit targeting → clears fair-lending).
