The PFI Verdict v0 — for calibration

The single rule that turns each account into a verdict: are we their primary financial institution, is it split, or has a competitor won them — inferred entirely from outbound activity, balances, and engagement.

The score

Primacy Score = 0.50 × Retention + 0.25 × Balance + 0.25 × Engagement

Confidence & when we abstain

  • Coverage gate. coverage_pct = classified outbound ÷ total outbound. Below ~30% → No verdict. We refuse to guess primacy on a book we can barely see.
  • Missing pillar. No balance or no engagement data → re-normalize weights, drop pfi_confidence to Medium/Low.
  • Output. Every account carries pfi_confidence = High / Medium / Low with a reason, never a bare score.

Rules & special cases

Ecosystems

Venmo / Zelle / Cash-App-P2P are excluded from primacy entirely — reported as a separate disintermediation signal. A rail is not a bank.

Neobanks

Cash App / PayPal balance / Chime count toward Retention only when outbound crosses the amount-gate (big / recurring = a parked balance); small flows stay conduit.

Product guard

Non-transacting products (CD / savings-only per Prod Desc) are not primacy candidates — excluded, or the Engagement pillar is dropped. Low card use there isn't disengagement, it's the design.

Personal vs business

Scored separately — business primacy behaves differently and shares no thresholds with consumer.

Cash

ATM withdrawals count in the denominator (total outflow) but are unattributed — they lower coverage, never assign a competitor.
